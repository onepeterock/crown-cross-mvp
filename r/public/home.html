The Academy

A school that knows each pupil by name and calls them upward.

Full Home Education is the heart of the Academy: a coordinated Academy Day in your own timezone, an age-appropriate pastoral cohort, live teaching by qualified faculty recruited from around the world, a living Learning Plan and Crown Pathway, and a weekly Parent Assurance written by people who teach your child.

Flexible Home Education, Private Tutoring and Advanced Learning remain connected pathways within one Academy, never a marketplace.

The Academy Day

Welcome, tutor presence, live lessons, preparation, independent study, assignments and reflection, ordered into a rhythm that makes the institution real for a child at a desk in Lagos, Dubai or Toronto.

Human authorship

Teachers observe, assess and write. Tools may recommend; accountable people decide. No grade, pathway change or safeguarding judgement is ever made by an algorithm.

Character and community

Daily reflection, termly virtues, service and assemblies belong naturally within Academy life. Families of every background and belief are welcomed as full members of the community.

Admissions

Places are reserved in small cohorts.

Cohorts open at six pupils and are held to eight. Admission follows enquiry, application, assessment, decision and offer, so that every child is placed with care rather than sold a seat.
