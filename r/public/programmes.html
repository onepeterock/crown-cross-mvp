Pathways

Four connected pathways, one Academy

Full Home Education is the core proposition. Flexible Home Education, Private Tutoring and Advanced Learning remain connected pathways within the same Academy, with the same faculty, records and care.

Pathway I

Full Home Education

The core Academy pathway: a complete, coordinated Academy Day

A coordinated Academy Day with an age-appropriate pastoral cohort, live teaching, assignments, community and character education; a formal CCOA Learning Plan, Crown Pathway and weekly Parent Assurance. Practice & Mastery is included.

  • ✦A complete Academy Day in the family's timezone
  • ✦Age-appropriate pastoral cohort with a form tutor
  • ✦Full UK curriculum, live teaching by qualified faculty
  • ✦CCOA Learning Plan, Crown Pathway and weekly Parent Assurance
  • ✦Daily tutor time, assemblies and reflection
  • ✦Practice & Mastery included

Pathway II

Flexible Home Education

A configured mix of subjects within the Academy rhythm

A configured selection of subjects, live teaching and Academy rhythm for families who combine the Academy with other provision. Resources, reporting and planning depth follow the approved programme; full-time entitlements are not assumed.

  • ✦Live teaching within the Academy Day rhythm
  • ✦Assignments, evidence and subject reporting
  • ✦Access to enrolled-subject Library resources

Private Tutoring & Supplementary Learning

Individual subject provision and authorised one-to-one sessions

Access to enrolled subjects, authorised tutoring sessions with approved Academy faculty, assigned resources and subject-scoped Practice & Mastery, with targeted evidence and reporting. Booking is Academy-controlled, never an open marketplace.

  • ✦Authorised one-to-one sessions with approved faculty
  • ✦Subject-scoped resources and Practice & Mastery
  • ✦Targeted evidence and reporting

Pathway IV

Advanced Learning

Human-approved greater depth, acceleration, challenge and mentoring

Advanced Scholar provision: greater depth, subject acceleration, challenge, mentoring and specialist review, approved by qualified academic staff on multiple sources of evidence. A pupil may be advanced in one subject while receiving support in another and remaining in an age-appropriate pastoral cohort.

  • ✦Reviewed, human-approved provision per subject
  • ✦Greater-depth curriculum and subject acceleration
  • ✦Mentoring and specialist review
  • ✦Advanced Scholar resources in the Learning Library
  • ✦Pastoral cohort unchanged
