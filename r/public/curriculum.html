The British curriculum, consistent worldwide

We follow the National Curriculum for England through Key Stages 1 to 3, then Cambridge, Pearson Edexcel and AQA specifications for IGCSE and A-Level, so a child who joins us in Nairobi and moves to Toronto continues exactly where they left off.

Key Stage 2 (Years 3-6, ages 7-11)

Subjects

English

  1. Term 1: Narrative writing and reading comprehension
    • Use fronted adverbials and expanded noun phrases
    • Retrieve and infer from fiction texts
    • Plan, draft and edit a short story
  2. Term 2: Non-fiction and persuasive writing
    • Identify features of persuasive texts
    • Use modal verbs and relative clauses
    • Write a balanced argument

History

Scheme of work follows the National Curriculum programme of study.

Mathematics

  1. Term 1: Place value and the four operations
    • Read, write and order numbers to 1,000,000
    • Add and subtract using formal written methods
    • Multiply up to 4 digits by 2 digits
    • Solve multi-step problems
  2. Term 2: Fractions, decimals and percentages
    • Compare and order fractions
    • Convert between fractions, decimals and percentages
    • Add and subtract fractions with different denominators
  3. Term 3: Geometry, measures and statistics
    • Measure and classify angles
    • Calculate area and perimeter
    • Interpret line graphs and tables
