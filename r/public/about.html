About us

A school with excellence and care at its heart

Crown & Cross Online Academy is a UK-based online school founded to give families anywhere in the world access to a rigorous British education, delivered with personal attention to every child.

Our mission

To form young people of curiosity, knowledge and character through excellent teaching, wherever their families live.

Our values

Community

We begin each day together with a tutor. Families of every background and belief are welcomed as full members of the Academy, and every child is known by name.

Excellence

British curriculum standards, qualified teachers and honest reporting. We aim for every child to exceed what they thought possible.

Global family

Our teachers come from Britain, Africa, Asia and Europe; our students from every continent. Difference is a gift, not a barrier.

Safeguarding

Every adult is background-checked and trained. Lessons are recorded, and parents can see everything in their portal.

Leadership

Our Principal and Director of Studies are experienced UK school leaders. The Academy is governed by a board that includes educators, professionals and parents, and is operated by Crown & Cross Online Academy Ltd, registered in England and Wales.

Our crest

The crown speaks of the British tradition of excellence; the cross marks the crossing of paths where teachers, pupils and families from across the world meet. Navy for depth and steadfastness, gold for the value of every child.
