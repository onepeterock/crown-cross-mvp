Pathway

Private Tutoring & Supplementary Learning

Individual subject provision and authorised one-to-one sessions

Access to enrolled subjects, authorised tutoring sessions with approved Academy faculty, assigned resources and subject-scoped Practice & Mastery, with targeted evidence and reporting. Booking is Academy-controlled, never an open marketplace.

What is included

  • ✦Authorised one-to-one sessions with approved faculty
  • ✦Subject-scoped resources and Practice & Mastery
  • ✦Targeted evidence and reporting
  • ✦Exam-board specific preparation where enrolled (IGCSE, A-Level)
  • ✦Academy-controlled booking with safeguarding oversight

How lessons run

Lessons are live on Zoom or Google Meet, scheduled in your local timezone and matched to a teacher whose working hours suit your family. Every class has a maximum of one student, with 4 hours of live teaching a week plus homework and termly written reports.

Begin an enquiry, tell us about your child, and admissions will guide you through assessment, decision and offer. Fees are confirmed in the offer.
