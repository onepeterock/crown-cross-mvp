FAQ

Questions families ask us

Is Crown & Cross a real school?+

Yes. We are a UK-based online school offering the British curriculum from Key Stage 1 to A-Level, with live lessons taught by qualified teachers. Students sit IGCSE and A-Level examinations as private candidates at approved centres in their country, and we guide families through the entry process.

Which countries can enrol?+

Any country. We currently teach families in over 30 countries across Africa, the Middle East, Europe, North America and Asia. Your timetable, reminders and billing all work in your local timezone and currency.

What time are lessons?+

Lessons are scheduled to suit the family's timezone and matched with teachers whose working hours align. Full-time students follow a school day in their local time; tutoring and group classes are arranged around local school hours.

Are your teachers qualified?+

Every teacher holds a recognised teaching qualification (for example QTS or PGCE in the UK, PGDE or B.Ed elsewhere), has a verified degree, and passes an enhanced background check before meeting a student.

How does payment work?+

Fees are set in pounds sterling and billed monthly per child by card through Stripe. You can pay in your local currency where supported. UK families pay 20% VAT; international families are not charged VAT.

Can I cancel?+

Yes. You can pause or cancel from the family portal at any time. Changes take effect at the next monthly billing date, and there are no cancellation fees.

Do you welcome families of every background?+

Yes. The Academy is open to families of every nationality, culture and belief. Our shared values are curiosity, knowledge and character, and every child is treated with equal respect.

What technology do we need?+

A laptop or tablet with a camera and microphone, a stable internet connection, and either Zoom or Google Meet. Homework can be uploaded as photos or PDFs from any device.

How do I become a teacher?+

Apply through the Teach with us page. You will upload your qualifications and identity documents, complete a background check, and tell us your preferred teaching hours. Teachers are paid a fixed monthly salary in their local currency.
