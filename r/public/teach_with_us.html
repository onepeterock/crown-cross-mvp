Careers

Teach the British curriculum from anywhere

We recruit qualified teachers from every country and pay a reliable monthly salary in your own currency. Teach live, online, to motivated students around the world.

What we look for

  • A recognised teaching qualification (QTS, PGCE, PGDE, B.Ed or national equivalent) and a relevant degree
  • At least two years of classroom experience; British curriculum or Cambridge/Edexcel experience preferred
  • Fluent English, a quiet teaching space, reliable internet and a camera
  • Willingness to complete an enhanced background check and safeguarding training
  • Sympathy with our Christian ethos

How the application works

  1. Apply online with your qualifications, subjects, country and preferred teaching hours (about 15 minutes)
  2. Screening call and a short demonstration lesson
  3. Document verification and background check
  4. Offer, contract and payout setup (Stripe, Wise or Paystack depending on your country)
  5. Onboarding, then your first assigned students
