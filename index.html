Crown & Cross Online Academy

An Education for Life

Clickable MVP of the Academy platform, Phase A. Choose a role and walk through its spaces exactly as the application renders them. Names shown inside are fictional demo data.

This is a static snapshot of the working application with seeded demo data (18 faculty in 8 countries, about 125 pupils in 14 countries, six months of records). Navigation works throughout; forms, buttons and sign-in are illustrative and do not save. The live application runs on Next.js and PostgreSQL and is in the project folder.
